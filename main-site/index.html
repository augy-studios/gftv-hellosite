How a HelloSpot works

  1. Spot the smile

    Look for the HelloSmile on a card, a lanyard or a sign. Wherever it is, that is a HelloSpot.

  2. Say hello

    Come over and say hi. Nobody at a HelloSpot expects you to know anyone yet.

  3. Make a friend

    Meet the people around you, and leave with someone to look out for at the next meet.

    Wait, what is GFTV?

    Global Furry Television

    Global Furry Television (GFTV) is Asia's biggest furry fandom news channel, based in Singapore.

    We showcase furries' growth for everyone today and tomorrow to appreciate, and we host spaces so people can learn about and understand each other.

    Spanning major continents from home, GFTV's team is international, multicultural and multilingual.
